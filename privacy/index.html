Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Your Information & The Games

This Privacy Policy explains how The Zodiac Box Games handles information when you use its promotional game website.

Information We Collect

We collect the TikTok username you enter so we can save your weekly game choices and any prizes you reveal. We also maintain game activity information such as which games were played, dates and times of play, game results, prizes won, prize claim or fulfillment status, and an associated TikTok Shop order reference when an administrator records one for fulfillment.

Why We Use It

We use this information to identify game participants, enforce the limit of two game choices per TikTok username per week, save game results, group multiple results under one participant, allow The Zodiac Box administrator to locate unclaimed gifts, fulfill verified promotional gifts with qualifying orders, troubleshoot the game system, and maintain promotion records.

TikTok Account Access

The Zodiac Box Games does not log into your TikTok account, request your TikTok password, post content for you, read private messages, or manage your TikTok account. You simply enter your TikTok username so your game record can be matched to your future TikTok Shop order.

Purchases

The Zodiac Box Games does not process TikTok Shop payments. Purchases, payment information, shipping information, taxes, and TikTok Shop transactions are handled through TikTok Shop and its applicable service providers. The game administrator may record an order reference solely to document fulfillment of a saved promotional gift.

How Information Is Stored

Game and participant records are stored using service providers used to operate The Zodiac Box Games, including its hosted database and website infrastructure. Reasonable access controls are used to limit administrative access. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

Information is not sold as part of The Zodiac Box Games. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the website, database, and game functionality, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the integrity of the promotion.

Retention and Requests

Game records may be retained as reasonably necessary for prize fulfillment, fraud prevention, recordkeeping, and operation of recurring promotions. A participant may contact The Zodiac Box through its official TikTok account or shop contact methods to ask about game information associated with their username or request deletion, subject to records that must reasonably be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or completed-transaction purposes.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the game system changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to The Zodiac Box through its official TikTok account or shop contact methods.
